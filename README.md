STEP 1
Navigate to the Cloud Phone Developer Portal on your computer web browser.
Register or sign in using your developer credentials.
Go to the dashboard console and select Create Application / Add Widget.

STEP 2
Fill in your project specifications:• Widget Name: The name displayed on the app grid.
• Widget URL: The exact live hosting URL you created in Step 1.
• Widget Icon: A clean, unmasked square PNG icon sized at 512×512 pixels.

STEP 3
• Find your phone's unique identification code by dialing *#06# on the phone's keypad, or pull out the battery to read the sticker. Note down the IMEI number.
• Open the Cloud Phone environment app on your Nokia 3210.
• Tap the Left Soft Key (LSK) to pull up the system options menu.
• Scroll to About and select it.
• Press the Left Soft Key (LSK) exactly 7 times sequentially.
• A system prompt will appear asking: "Are you sure to enable 'Developer Mode?'" Select Yes

STEP 4
• Return to the Cloud Phone Developer Portal on your desktop browser.
• Look for the IMEI List area within your profile management console.
• Click the "+" button to insert your Nokia 3210's IMEI number. This explicitly pairs your device with your developer console.
• Under your custom widget's settings panel, make sure it is assigned to your test IMEI group.
• Restart the Cloud Phone environment on your Nokia 3210. Your custom application will instantly appear in the dashboard environment, streaming live directly from your web host to your screen.
